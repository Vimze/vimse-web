# Vimse Marketing Website — Vision & Goals

**Last updated:** May 3, 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

The Vimse website at `nesh.no` is the public face of the Vimse app. Its job is to make people aware the app exists, understand what it does, and feel confident enough to download it.

It is **not** the web client (that will live at `app.nesh.no` later). It is a marketing, information, and SEO surface — a shop window that draws people in.

---

## 2. Goals

### 2.1 Discoverability

- Rank in search engines for queries around meal planning, offline shopping lists, recipe management, and life organization.
- Provide clear, structured content that AI assistants (Google AI Overview, ChatGPT, Perplexity, etc.) can reference when users ask about apps that solve these problems.
- Serve as a canonical link target for app store listings, social media, and any future press or content marketing.

### 2.2 Conversion

- Give visitors a clear, fast path to download the app (App Store and Play Store links prominent on every page).
- Communicate the value of Vimse within seconds of landing — headline, visuals, and CTA above the fold.
- Reduce friction: no sign-up required to learn about the app, no pop-ups, fast page loads.

### 2.3 Trust & quality perception

- Look polished and professional — visitors should feel the app is built by someone who cares about quality.
- Consistent visual identity with the app itself (same colors, typography, design language).
- Clearly communicate what the app does and does not do (honest, no overselling).

### 2.4 Foundation for growth

- Structured so a blog, changelog, or help center can be added later without rearchitecting.
- Ready for localization (English first, Norwegian possible later).
- Ready for the web client to coexist on a subdomain without conflicts.

---

## 3. Target audience

- **Primary:** People who cook at home and want a better way to manage recipes and shopping lists — typically 25–45, smartphone users, slightly frustrated with scattered notes and generic apps.
- **Secondary:** Anyone who values offline-first tools — travelers, people with unreliable connectivity, privacy-conscious users who prefer local-first data.

---

## 4. What the website should communicate

### 4.1 Core message

> Vimse helps you organize your everyday life — starting with what you eat and what you buy — and it works whether you have internet or not.

### 4.2 Key value propositions

| Value | What to say | Why it matters to visitors |
|-------|-------------|---------------------------|
| **Offline-first** | Works without internet, syncs when you reconnect | Reliability in the kitchen, in the store, while traveling |
| **Recipes + shopping in one place** | Add recipe ingredients directly to a shopping list | Solves the "copy ingredients from recipe to notes" pain |
| **Cross-platform** | iOS, Android, web (coming) | Use it on whichever device is closest |
| **Fast and focused** | Not a social media app; no ads, no bloat | Respects your time and attention |
| **Private** | Your data stays yours; no tracking-based business model | Trust and peace of mind |

### 4.3 Feature highlights to showcase

- Create and manage recipes with ingredients, steps, and photos
- Shopping lists with checking, reordering, and archiving
- One-tap "add recipe ingredients to shopping list"
- Servings adjustment recalculates quantities
- Works fully offline, then syncs seamlessly
- Future: packing lists, travel planning, todo lists, wish lists

---

## 5. Content by page

### 5.1 Landing page (index)

The front door. Job: hook attention, communicate value, and get the visitor to either download or explore further.

- **Hero:** Compelling headline + subtitle + app store CTAs + device mockup
- **Benefits:** 3–4 short value propositions with icons
- **Feature highlights:** 2–3 sections showing what the app actually looks like and does
- **Final CTA:** One more push to download

### 5.2 Features page

A deeper look for visitors who want to understand exactly what the app does before committing.

- Detailed breakdown of each feature area (recipes, shopping, offline, cross-platform)
- Visual examples or screenshots for each
- "Coming soon" section for planned features (creates anticipation)
- CTA at the bottom

### 5.3 About page (includes contact)

Who is behind this, and how to get in touch.

- The story: why Vimse exists, what problem it was born from
- The philosophy: offline-first, privacy-respecting, focused
- Contact information (email)
- Optional: link to social/community if that exists later

---

## 6. What the website is NOT

- **Not a web client.** Users cannot log in, manage lists, or use the app on this site. That comes later at `app.nesh.no`.
- **Not a blog (yet).** Content marketing may come, but the initial site is pure product marketing and information.
- **Not a community or forum.** Social features live in the app, not here.
- **Not multilingual (yet).** English first. Norwegian can be added later if needed.

## 8. Design principles for the website

1. **Speed over spectacle.** Pages load instantly. Animations enhance, never block.
2. **Clarity over cleverness.** A visitor should understand what Vimse does within 5 seconds of landing.
3. **Consistency with the app.** Same colors, same type, same rounded friendly feel. The website should feel like it was made by the same hand that made the app.
4. **Mobile-first.** Most visitors discovering an app will be on their phone. Design for that first.
5. **Honest and human.** No corporate fluff, no fake testimonials. Say what it is, show what it does.

---

## 9. Tone of voice

- Friendly but not childish
- Confident but not arrogant
- Clear and direct — short sentences, active voice
- Slightly informal — first person plural ("we built this because...") is fine
- Norwegian warmth: approachable, genuine, no hard sell
